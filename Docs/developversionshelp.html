Develop Settings Versions

Why did my settings change?

Winnow keeps improving its develop adjustments. Sometimes an improvement changes what a slider value means: a slider gets a wider range, or an adjustment is retuned so the same number now looks a little different.

Your edits are a recipe of slider values, saved in the image's sidecar. When you open an image whose recipe was made by an older version of Winnow, its values are converted automatically so the picture keeps its look under the new adjustment. You do not need to do anything.

When this has happened to the image you are developing, a line at the top of the Develop panel says Settings updated for this version of Winnow. Hover over it to see what changed, or click Dismiss to hide it for that image.

What you might notice

A slider may sit at a different number than you remember. That is the conversion: the new number gives the same result the old one did.

Where an old look cannot be reproduced exactly, the conversion gets as close as it can. Each change is listed below, so you know which adjustments to check.

Is my sidecar changed?

The converted recipe is saved the next time the image's sidecar is written: when you edit it, or when Winnow refreshes its preview. After that it opens without conversion. An older version of Winnow can still read it, but will say it was written by a newer version and ignore anything it does not understand.

Presets and copied settings are converted the same way, each time they are applied.

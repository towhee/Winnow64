Undo and Redo

Edit > Undo (Cmd+Z on a Mac, Ctrl+Z on Windows and Linux) takes back your last change. Edit > Redo (Cmd+Shift+Z, or Ctrl+Y on Windows) puts it back. The menu item names the step, for example "Undo Rating (12 images)".

A change made to several images at once is one step. Undo puts every one of those images back the way it was.

What you can undo while browsing

  • Pick, reject, unpick, Clear all picks, and Reject unpicked, clear picks.
  • Ratings and color labels.
  • Title, creator, copyright, email and URL typed in the Metadata panel.
  • Rotate left and rotate right.
  • Keywords added to or removed from images, including images dropped on a keyword.
  • Duplicate OK.
  • A location set by dropping images on the map. Undo puts back the location the images had before, including the camera's own.

Collections, places, versions and keywords

  • Collections and queries: new, rename, duplicate, delete, move, edit a query, and adding or removing images. A deleted collection comes back with everything inside it and all its images.
  • Places on the map: new, edit, rename and delete.
  • Versions: new, rename, delete and Set Version as Master. A deleted version comes back with its develop settings, its History and its collections.
  • The Keyword list: adding keywords, Remove from list, synonyms, Include on Export, Build from catalog and Import.

Files

  • Delete and Delete rejected: undo brings the images back from the Trash (Recycle Bin on Windows) with their sidecars, collections, picks, rejects and Develop History. If you empty the Trash first, they cannot come back.
  • Paste: undo moves the pasted files to the Trash.
  • Rename: undo gives every renamed file its old name back.
  • In the Library: moving or copying images onto a folder in the Filters panel, and renaming or moving a folder.

If a file is not where the step left it, or its old name is now taken, Winnow says so and the step stays in the Edit menu. Deleting a folder, creating a folder, and dragging files between folders in the Folders or Bookmarks panels cannot be undone yet.

Renaming or moving a keyword in the Keyword list, Remove everywhere, Tidy, and merging an unfiled keyword change keywords in your image files too. These cannot be undone yet.

Undo writes the earlier value back to the sidecar (or into the image file when "Permit image file modification" is on), exactly as the change was written.

After you change folders

Undo still works after you open another folder. The images are found by their file, and their sidecars are changed directly. A rotation is the exception: it is undone only while its images are loaded, and Winnow tells you if any were skipped.

In Develop

In Develop, Cmd+Z steps back through the image's History, and Cmd+Shift+Z steps forward. While you paint a Brush or Object mask, Cmd+Z takes back your last stroke first. See the History page.

Typing

When the cursor is in a text field, Cmd+Z undoes your typing instead.

What is kept

The last 200 steps are kept until you quit. Undo is not saved between sessions.

Making a new change after an undo discards the steps you could have redone.

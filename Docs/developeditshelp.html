Edits

What it does

Edits is the top of the Develop dock, and it answers one question: what do the sliders below apply to? Every image starts with a single scope called Global, which covers the whole frame. Add a mask and you get a second scope — Mask 1 — with its own complete set of Basic, Curves, Color, Calibrate, Color Grade and Effects values, applied only where the mask says.

Selecting a scope in this list is what points the panels below at it. The section headers do not change; their values do.

The list

Each row is one scope:

  • The name — Global, or Mask 1, Mask 2 ... , which you can rename to something meaningful (Sky, Face, Foreground). Click a row to make it active.
  • The eye shows or hides that scope's whole contribution — a one-click before / after of everything in it, with no values lost.
  • The [⋮] menu carries the actions for that scope: modify its mask (M), reset it, rename it, delete it. Global cannot be renamed or deleted and has no mask; its menu offers New mask (N) and Reset Global.

The band at the top of the list has the same pair. Its eye acts on the selected scope, and its menu adds New mask, Reset all edits — which wipes the entire recipe, every scope, the crop and the spot heals, plus the history and the sidecar record, on every selected image, after a confirmation — and the Edits layout picker.

Scopes stack

Scopes are applied in list order: Global first, then each mask on top of the result. Two masks that overlap both act in the overlap, in order. So the usual shape of an edit is a global rendering that is right for the frame as a whole, then masks that fix what the frame as a whole could not.

Layouts

Edits layout in the band menu chooses how the list and the editor are arranged:

  • Flat — the scope list, with the editor below the whole list.
  • Nested — the editor sits under the selected scope's own row, so the sliders are visibly inside the scope they belong to.
  • Minimal — no list at all: one dropdown, a [+] for a new mask, and the editor. The narrowest layout; in it the bar's [⋮] is the only menu in the panel, so it carries the panel actions too.

Masks

A mask scope is built from one or more submasks — gradients, a brush, colour and luminance ranges, and the AI selections (Subject, Sky, Background, Depth, Object) — combined in the Submasks list nested under the scope. Press N for a new mask, M to modify the active mask's, and O to show or hide the mask overlay on the image. The Submasks band has its own help page.

Multiple images

Develop edits apply to the whole selection, not just the image on screen: adjust a slider with several thumbnails selected and the same change lands on all of them. Masks are the exception — a mask belongs to the pixels of one image, so it never travels. That is also why a preset or Copy / Paste Settings always merges onto scope Global.

Related

  • History dock — the session's snapshots of this image's recipe.
  • Presets dock (P) — saved recipes, merged onto the active scope.
  • Copy / Paste Settings (Cmd/Ctrl+Opt+C, Cmd/Ctrl+Opt+V) — the same mechanism as a preset, unnamed.

What is saved

The whole scope list — names, enabled flags, every scope's values and every mask's submasks — is the develop recipe, written to the image's sidecar. The original file is never modified.

Winnow Overview

Overview

Winnow is for looking through your photos quickly, picking the keepers and setting the rest aside. You can rate them, label them, add keywords, develop raw files and copy your picks off a memory card.

There are two ways to see your photos in Winnow: Folders and Library. You switch between them with the Library | Folders buttons at the top left of the window.

Folders

Folders works like browsing your computer. Choose a folder and Winnow shows the photos in it, much as Finder or File Explorer would, but much faster and with the full-size picture a keystroke away.

  • You see one folder at a time, or a folder and the folders inside it when Include subfolders is on.
  • It is the natural choice for a memory card, a new download, or any folder you want to go through from start to finish.
  • Nothing needs to be set up first. Pick a folder and you are looking at it.

Library

Library shows all your photos together, wherever they are kept, as one collection. Instead of remembering which folder something is in, you narrow the whole Library down to what you want: a date, a camera, a rating, a keyword, a place.

  • It is the natural choice for questions like “all my five-star photos from last summer” or “every photo of the lake”, when the answer is spread across many folders.
  • Collections and Queries belong to the Library. A collection is a set of photos you put together by hand; a query is a saved search that stays up to date as photos are added. In Folders their panels are greyed out, with a note saying why.

How photos get into the Library

You choose which folders make up the Library. Switch to Library, then choose File > Manage Catalog... and add the folders that hold your photos, for example your main Pictures folder. Winnow reads them in the background while you keep working, and checks them again for new or removed photos when you open the Library.

Only those folders are in the Library. You can still open any other folder in Folders, such as a memory card, to look through it, but it is not added to the Library.

Winnow asks you about this the first time you open a folder of photos, and if you open the Library before anything is in it, Winnow takes you straight to Manage Catalog.

What works the same in both

  • Filters and search. The Filters panel narrows down whatever you are looking at: one folder in Folders, everything in Library. Its title tells you which: Filters (Folders) or Filters (Library). Press F2 to type a search.
  • Keywords, ratings and labels. These are saved with the photos themselves, so they are the same whichever way you are looking.
  • Places. Areas you draw on the map pick out the photos taken there, in either view.

Switching

Use the Library | Folders buttons at the top left, or View > Library and View > Folders. File > Open Library also takes you to the Library. Switching never changes or moves your photos; it only changes what you are looking at.

Spot Removal

What it does

Spot Removal takes things out of a photo: dust, blemishes, a stray twig, a wire, a leash, a small object. Every repair is a recipe entry, like the rest of Develop: the original is never touched, and a repair can be removed at any time.

Click the Spot button in the Develop action row and choose a tool. Press S to turn the tool on or off again in the tool you used last.

To turn the tool off, press Esc on the image, choose Turn off spot tool in the Spot menu, or choose the checked tool again.

The five tools

Spot

Click a blemish. Winnow finds a matching area nearby and heals the spot from it. Best for dust and small skin or sensor marks.

Heal

Brush over a flaw. Winnow picks a source area and blends its texture into the tone around the flaw, so the repair takes on the local brightness and colour.

A white ring marks the source, joined to the repair by a dashed line. Drag the ring to take the texture from somewhere else. Hovering either end outlines the brushed area at both places, so you can line the source up.

Clone

Like Heal, but the source is copied exactly as it is, tone and all. Use it when the blend of Heal smears an edge or a pattern that must line up. Drag the source ring the same way.

Fill

For things that are long, thin or scattered: a wire across the sky, a twig, a leash. Paint over it in as many strokes as you need, then press Return. Winnow fills the painted area piece by piece, each piece from its own nearby source.

Object

For a whole object. Paint over it completely, with a little margin, then press Return. The area is replaced by an AI (regenerative) fill. The first time, Winnow offers to download the fill model.

Cover the object entirely. Anything left showing at the edge can guide the fill back into the shape you are removing.

While painting

[ and ] (or a two-finger drag) change the brush size.

In Fill and Object, hold Opt/Alt while painting to erase part of the painted area. Esc clears the painting before you commit it.

Each repair leaves a pin. Click a pin to remove that repair.

Esc turns the tool off.

Checking your work

Uncheck Show repairs in the Spot menu to see the photo without any of its repairs; check it again to bring them back. Nothing is removed.

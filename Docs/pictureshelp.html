Developed, Original and Camera

Every image can be shown as up to three pictures. The status bar shows which one you are looking at. Click it for the options below.

Developed

The image with your edits from the Develop workflow applied. An image you have not edited has no separate developed picture, so it shows as Original or Camera.

Original

The image with no edits: the file itself for a JPG, TIFF or PNG. In Develop, Before shows the Original of a raw file, rendered by Winnow with no adjustments.

Camera

The JPEG preview your camera embedded in a raw file, as the camera rendered it. JPG, TIFF and PNG files have no separate camera picture: their Camera picture is the Original.

When browsing, Winnow shows a raw file's Camera picture rather than decoding the raw. That keeps browsing fast. Winnow decodes the raw itself in Develop.

Show Edits (Y): all images

Shows or hides your edits for every image, in the thumbnails and the loupe. While edits are hidden, the status bar turns amber and says edits hidden, so the setting is never left on by accident. Edits are always shown in Develop.

Compare (\): this image only

Swaps the current image in the loupe to its other picture: Developed against Camera (raw) or Original (other files). Press \ again, or move to another image, to go back. Nothing else changes, and nothing is saved.

Opt/Alt + \ compares with the Camera picture. In Develop, \ is Before / After.

The amber corner badge on a thumbnail means the image has edits, whether or not they are being shown.

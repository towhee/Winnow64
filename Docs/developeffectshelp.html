Effects

What it does

Effects holds two finishing controls that work on the rendered image, after demosaic and after the tone and colour work: a vignette and film grain. Like every other section it is per scope, so either can be confined to a mask.

Noise reduction used to live here. It now sits in the Detail panel alongside Sharpening, because the two are one decision — noise reduction removes exactly the detail sharpening puts back.

Vignette

  • Vignette is corner exposure in stops (EV), ±5.00: negative darkens the corners (the familiar look), positive brightens them, which is how you correct a lens that falls off.
  • Feather is how far the falloff reaches in toward the centre. Low values keep it in the corners; high values make it a broad, soft dome.

The vignette is radial about the image centre. For an off-centre or shaped darkening — around a subject that is not in the middle — use a radial gradient mask and put an exposure adjustment in that scope instead.

Grain

Monochromatic grain added to luminance, which is what film grain actually is. Size and Roughness are sub-controls of Amount and do nothing until Amount is above zero.

  • Grain — the strength.
  • Size — particle size, fine to coarse. It is scaled to the image, so what you see on the editing proxy is what you get at full resolution.
  • Roughness — how patchy and irregular the grain is. Low is an even sprinkle; high clumps it, which reads as a faster, older stock.

Grain is also a practical tool, not only a look: a little of it hides banding in smooth skies and disguises the plastic texture that heavy noise reduction leaves behind.

Working with the sliders

  • Double-click a slider returns it to its default (Feather 50, Size 25, Roughness 50, Vignette and Grain 0).
  • Click a slider and the arrow keys nudge one unit, Page Up / Page Down ten.
  • The section's [⋮] menu has Show Effects changes (a before / after that keeps your values) and Reset Effects.
  • The header reads Effects * when anything in the panel is off its default.

Where it sits in the pipeline

Both run late, on the rendered image: the vignette, then sharpening (in the Detail panel), then grain last of all — grain belongs on top of the finished picture, not underneath a vignette that would modulate it, and not under a sharpener that would crisp it up.

What is saved

All five values are part of the develop recipe, stored per scope in the sidecar, and travel with presets, Copy / Paste Settings, History and multi-image editing.

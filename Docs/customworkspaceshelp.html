Custom Workspaces

A custom workspace is a panel layout you have saved under a name, so you can switch to it instantly.

Each custom workspace belongs to the workflow it was saved in: Browse, Develop, Keywords, Map, Embellish or Slide Show.

Opening one switches to its workflow (Develop enters Develop mode) and shows your layout instead of the workflow's usual one.

Opening a Custom Workspace

Press Ctrl/Cmd 0 to Ctrl/Cmd 9, or choose it at the bottom of Window > Workspace.

Or right-click a workflow in the Modes panel at the top of the window. Its menu lists the custom workspaces that belong to it.

The custom workspace stays in use until you switch to another workflow, or click the workflow's own button or key again. Reset Layout (Ctrl/Cmd Shift R) puts it back if a panel has moved.

Adding a Custom Workspace

Arrange the panels, then choose Window > Workspace > New Custom Workspace, or right-click the current workflow in the Modes panel and choose Add current layout.

Up to ten custom workspaces can be saved.

Changing a Custom Workspace

Open the custom workspace, rearrange the panels, then choose Window > Workspace > Update Custom Workspace.

This Dialog

The table has one row per custom workspace.

Rename custom workspace: click a name to rename it.

Window *: when ticked, the workspace also moves and sizes the Winnow window as it was when saved, and it is flagged with an asterisk in the Workspace menu. When not ticked only the panels change.

Delete: tick the workspaces to delete. Deleting cannot be undone.

Nothing changes until you press Update, which applies the new names, the Window * choices and the deletions, then closes.

Closing the window any other way (Esc, or its close button) discards your edits.

Two workspaces cannot share a name, and a name cannot be blank. Update says which name to fix.
